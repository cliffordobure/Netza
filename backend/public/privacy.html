Privacy Policy

Tajira Kenya mobile app and store · Last updated 17 September 2026

This page is the privacy policy Google Play and customers should use. It describes how Tajira Kenya handles personal data when you use the Tajira Android app or related services.

1. Who we are

Tajira Kenya (“Tajira”, “we”, “us”) is a Kenya-based store for electrical, CCTV, and related products. We operate the Tajira mobile app and admin systems from Nairobi, Kenya.

Questions about this policy: support@tajira.co.ke

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the store and your account:

  • Account details — name, phone number, email (if you add one), password (stored as a hash), optional gender, date of birth, and profile photo.
  • Delivery details — county, city, street, and a contact phone for each saved address.
  • Orders and shopping — cart items, orders, quotes, loyalty points, referrals, flash-drop and competition activity.
  • Payments — order amount, payment method (for example M-Pesa via Pesapal), and payment status. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or full card number.
  • Photos you upload — images you choose from the camera or gallery for quotes or your profile. Those files are stored so we can show them back to you.
  • Support and SMS — messages you send us, and promotional or order SMS we send to the phone on your account.
  • App use — the screen you are on and a session identifier so we can keep you signed in and see that the app is working. We do not collect precise GPS location. Delivery addresses are the locations you type in.
  • Device and network — IP address and basic request logs needed to secure the service and diagnose errors.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Process orders, quotes, delivery, returns, and customer support.
  • Take payment through Pesapal (including M-Pesa) and confirm that a payment succeeded.
  • Send transactional and promotional SMS through our SMS provider (Beem), including stock and offer messages to technicians or other customers when an admin sends them.
  • Run loyalty points, referrals, flash drops, and competitions.
  • Improve the app, prevent fraud, and keep the service available.

4. Permissions the Android app may ask for

  • Internet — to load the catalog, sign in, place orders, and pay.
  • Photos / storage — only when you pick an image for a quote or profile photo.
  • Camera — only if you choose to take a photo inside the app.

We do not use contacts, microphone, SMS inbox, or precise location permissions.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with processors who help us operate the store:

  • Pesapal — to start and confirm payments.
  • Beem Africa — to send SMS to the phone number on your account.
  • Cloudinary — to store images you upload.
  • Hosting providers — to run the API and database (currently including Render and MongoDB Atlas).
  • Delivery partners — name, phone, and address needed to deliver an order.

We may also disclose information if Kenyan law requires it, or to protect customers and the store from fraud or abuse.

6. Retention

We keep account, order, and payment records for as long as you have an account and as needed for accounting, dispute handling, and Kenyan legal requirements. You can ask us to close your account and delete data that we are not required to keep.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed. Access to admin tools is limited to authorised staff. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children

Tajira is a retail store app. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion where the law allows, and information about how we use it. Email support@tajira.co.ke to make a request.

You can also update your name, phone, addresses, and profile photo in the app, or stop using promotional SMS by asking support to remove you from marketing lists.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
