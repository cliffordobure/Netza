Delete your Tajira Kenya account

Tajira Kenya mobile app · Account and data deletion

This page is for customers of the Tajira Android app by Tajira Kenya who want their account and associated personal data deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@tajira.co.ke from the email on your account, or include the phone number you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Tajira account.
  3. We will confirm it is you, then delete the account.

Email a deletion request

We aim to complete deletion within 14 days of confirming the request.

What we delete

  • Your name, phone number, email, password, profile photo, and saved addresses
  • Your cart, quotes, loyalty points, referrals, and competition or flash-drop activity
  • Support messages and marketing SMS lists tied to your account
  • Sign-in sessions so you can no longer use the app with that account

What we keep, and for how long

We keep order and payment records that Kenyan tax, accounting, or dispute rules require. Those records stay for up to 7 years, then are removed or anonymised. They are not used to contact you or to keep a shopping account open.
